# Controlled vocabulary

Every ring in the catalogue, every component model and every research record uses these ids.
New ids may be added (see `docs/guides/adding-components.md`), never renamed silently.

## Stone materials (`stone.material`)

diamond, lab-diamond, moissanite, sapphire, ruby, emerald, aquamarine, morganite, alexandrite, tanzanite,
spinel, tourmaline, garnet, opal, peridot, topaz, amethyst, citrine, zircon, pearl, onyx

## Cuts (`stone.cut`)

round-brilliant, princess, cushion, oval, emerald-cut, asscher, radiant, pear, marquise, heart, trillion,
baguette, trapezoid, tapered-baguette, rose-cut, old-european, old-mine, hexagon, kite, shield, cabochon

## Heads / mounts (`head`)

solitaire-4-prong, solitaire-6-prong, 3-prong, double-claw, basket, cathedral-head, v-prong, bezel-full,
bezel-half, halo-round, halo-cushion, halo-oval, hidden-halo, double-halo, three-stone, five-stone, cluster,
trellis, tension, flush-gypsy, split-prong, compass, vintage-filigree, toi-et-moi, bypass-head, bezel-polygon,
bezel-cushion, halo-hexagon, deco-plaque, cluster-flank, tension-bypass
