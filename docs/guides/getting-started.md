# Getting started

Ring Creator lets you design an engagement ring and a matching wedding band, then look at both in 3D. Everything runs in your browser, and your designs are saved on your device.

## Build your first ring

1. **Open the builder.** Choose **Build** in the top bar. A simple solitaire appears so you always start from something that works.
2. **Pick the stone.** On the stone step choose a cut such as round brilliant or emerald, then set the carat weight, the gem, its color and its clarity. The ring updates as you go.
3. **Choose the head and shank.** The head holds the stone (prongs, bezel, halo) and the shank is the part of the ring around your finger. Try a [halo](#/components/head/halo-round) head with a plain shank, then swap in something with side stones.
4. **Pick metals and a finish.** Set the metal for each part. Mixing metals, such as rose gold on a white gold ring, is allowed.
5. **Add the wedding band.** Open the band step and choose a style. Contour bands bend around the head so the two rings sit flush. Use the toolbar to switch between ring, band and both together.
6. **Save and share.** Give the design a name and choose **Save**. Saved designs appear under **Saved**, where you can reopen, compare or export them.
