## Takeaways

- **Modern solitaire settings are cheap, the stone is not.** Platinum six-prong settings at Blue Nile run $1,315 to $1,510 for 1.8 to 2.0mm bands ([classic](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/classic-six-prong-solitaire-engagement-ring-in-platinum-item-194157), [petite](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/petite-nouveau-six-prong-solitaire-engagement-ring-in-platinum-item-194356)). Tiffany's Setting starts at $1,650 and Harry Winston's emerald-cut solitaire at $15,600 including a 1.00 ct stone ([Tiffany](https://www.tiffany.com/engagement/shop/engagement-rings/platinum/price-2000-5000), [Harry Winston](https://www.harrywinston.com/en/solitaire-emerald-cut-engagement-ring)).
- **Thin bands dominate.** Solitaire shanks seen here are 1.3 to 2.5mm wide; the bezel at 2.5mm was the widest ([Blue Nile bezel](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/bezel-solitaire-engagement-ring-in-18k-yellow-gold-item-296646)).
- **Hidden halos** keep a plain top view with 14 to 68 melee under the head; Brilliant Earth pages list setting prices of $1,190 to $1,690 ([Freesia](https://www.brilliantearth.com/Freesia-Hidden-Halo-Diamond-Ring-Rose-Gold-BE1D3939-19424931/?is_tab=1), [Gala](https://www.brilliantearth.com/Gala-Diamond-Ring-14K-White-Gold-BE1D6362P-76788400/)).
- **Art Deco (c.1920 to 1935):** platinum, old European cuts from 0.3 to 1.75 ct, openwork filigree, milgrain edges and geometric shoulders ([Filigree Jewelers](https://filigreejewelers.com/products/antique-engagement-ring-art-deco-70-old-european-cut-diamond-platinum)). The one priced piece asked $15,700 ([Erstwhile](https://erstwhilejewelry.com/collections/archive/products/diamond-within-a-diamond-art-deco-engagement-ring)).
- **Victorian and Georgian (1800s to c.1900):** old mine stones of 1.0 to 2.3 ct in rose gold with silver tops, often bezels, clusters or rose-cut halos ([1stDibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/jewelry/rings/engagement-rings/victorian-228-carats-old-mine-cut-diamond-14k-rose-gold-silver-bezel-set-ring/id-j_18630772), [Wilson's](https://www.wilsonsestatejewelry.com/en-de/products/georgian-2-00-ctw-diamond-silver-topped-14-karat-gold-engagement-ring-circa-1800s)). One Victorian solitaire sold at $7,000 ([Erstwhile](https://erstwhilejewelry.com/products/victorian-1-00-carat-old-mine-cushion-diamond-antique-engagement-ring)).
- **Mid-century (1950s):** platinum crown baskets with split prongs and pierced cathedral shoulders, or small two-tone illusion heads ([Wilson's](https://wilsonsestatejewelry.com/en-de/products/mid-century-2-31-ctw-old-european-cut-diamond-platinum-vintage-crown-solitaire-engagement-ring-gia), [Antique Jewelry Mall](https://www.antiquejewelrymall.com/products/r844)).
- **Famous rings** vary widely in stone, from a 33.19 ct Asscher ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krupp_Diamond)) to a 12 ct oval sapphire halo ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engagement_ring_of_Catherine,_Princess_of_Wales)). Sources disagree on Grace Kelly's carat (10.48 vs 10.38) ([Vrai](https://www.vrai.com/journal/post/grace-kellys-engagement-rings)).
- **Bands:** milgrain platinum bands run 2.5 to 4mm ([Vrai $1,495](https://www.vrai.com/wedding-band/milgrain-band/platinum-42533981061213), [Tiffany Together $2,100](https://www.1stdibs.com/jewelry/rings/wedding-rings/tiffany-co-together-platinum-4mm-double-milgrain-band-wedding-ring/id-j_24178302)).

## Caveats

- Several URLs (Jackie Kennedy, Diana) were read as search summaries or a redirected page, not confirmed primary sources.
- Many antique listings are sold; prices there are asks or null.
