## Takeaways

Everything below is drawn from the 33 records and the pages linked above.

**Setting prices (centre stone excluded).** Platinum halo and hidden-halo settings on Blue Nile cluster at $2,550-$4,300 ([Crown Pavé](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/crown-pave-hidden-halo-diamond-engagement-ring-in-platinum-by-james-allen-item-315147), [Heiress](https://www.bluenile.com/ca/build-your-own-ring/blue-nile-studio-heiress-halo-platinum-engagement-ring_50575), [Royal Crown](https://www.bluenile.com/mx/build-your-own-ring/royal-crown-halo-diamond-engagement-ring-platinum_70985)). Double halos and heavily pave'd shanks run $6,765-$12,560. 14K gold versions sit at $1,450-$3,040. Designer houses start far higher: Harry Winston from $11,200 (single micropavé) and $28,000 (double halo).

**Halo sizes.** Pages rarely give halo width. Stone counts are the usable signal: 12 halo diamonds plus 6 shoulder stones on Royal Crown, and about 120 diamonds on the double-halo Gala (on a 1 ct centre). A 1/3-3/8 ct tw single halo is the common "entry" figure for 1 ct centres on Blue Nile; the hidden halo ([Bamboo](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/bamboo-hidden-halo-side-stone-diamond-engagement-ring-with-halo-in-14k-rose-gold-by-james-allen-item-316207)) lists 34 diamonds, about 1/3 ct tw, for a 1 ct centre.

**Side-stone ratios.** Trilogy settings are specified by side-stone total weight, not per stone: 1/3 ct tw on a 0.80+ ct centre ([Bella Vaughan trapezoid](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/bella-vaughan-trapezoid-three-stone-engagement-ring-in-platinum-1-3-ct-tw-item-192098)), 5/8 ct tw tapered baguettes for 2-8 ct centres ([Blue Nile](https://www.bluenile.com/mo/en/build-your-own-ring/three-stone-tapered-baguette-diamond-engagement-ring-platinum_81169)), and 1 ct tw trapezoids for 2-8 ct centres. A pear-sided trilogy ([Opera](https://www.brilliantearth.com/Opera-Three-Stone-Diamond-Ring-14K-White-Gold-BE1D5320-76823401/)) starts at 0.37 ct tw. Five-stone: VRAI's Heirloom uses 0.25 ct + 0.10 ct baguettes per side (0.70 ctw), graduating outward ([VRAI](https://www.vrai.com/engagement-rings/five-stone-ring/oval/platinum)).

**Band widths.** Shanks are slim: 1.4-1.9 mm for most halo, cluster and split settings, 2.0-2.5 mm for trilogies and twisted bands, 3.25 mm on the Gala double halo. Matching bands are 1.5-2.0 mm.

**Shank styles.** Split shanks appear as pave-lined splits ([Tressa](https://www.brilliantearth.com/Sapphire-Tressa-Split-Shank-Diamond-Ring-14K-White-Gold-BE1D2014-SBSL8X6OV2/), [Blue Nile](https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/split-shank-halo-diamond-engagement-ring-in-14k-white-gold-item-203862)) and X-crossovers. Bypass shanks pair with kite-set or floral heads.

**Carat range.** Most retailer settings accept 0.20/0.50/0.80 up to 5-8 ct centres. Real examples with stated carats are few: Soleste resale 1.08 ct, Diana 12 ct sapphire, Jackie 2.84 + 2.88 ct.

**Curved bands.** Brilliant Earth sells separate contour bands (Lunette, Yvette, Chamise) at 1.5-1.7 mm, with Yvette at $1,590.

## Vocabulary gaps and doubts

- Gaps: no `bamboo`/`knot`/`kite-set` shank or head ids; no cathedral-style halo head separate from `cathedral-head`; no `euro-shank`; no `hidden-halo` for band profile; `lab-diamond` fits VRAI but "created" origin is not tracked as an accent. Band `stones` values are free text, so a "pear + round" tag would be useful.
- Doubtful records: r2-025 (Wikipedia URL fetched showed Diana's table under Kate's article title), r2-026 and r2-027 (cut and metal from secondary outlets), r2-028 (page now 410, summary only), r2-016 (metal and carat range unclear), r2-009 (oval vs round centre).
