## Takeaways

- Fetched in full: the Point No Point Studio, Camelliarts, Etsy, Aquamarise, Washington Diamond and Robinsons pages. Retailers such as Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth and Catbird blocked fetches or returned no usable product data, so they are thin here.
- Cuts by gem: sapphire and ruby (Mohs 9) appear in oval, kite and emerald-cut, with teal sapphire almost always oval. Aquamarine and morganite (Mohs 7.5-8) suit big step cuts and kites. Opal (about 5.5-6.5, general knowledge) is nearly always a cabochon in a bezel or halo, which protects the edges. Salt-and-pepper diamonds are mostly rose-cut hexagon or emerald shapes. Moissanite (about 9.25) shows up in round and kite cuts. Hardness figures are general gemology knowledge, not from the listings.
- Color names seen: teal, blue-green, yellow-green, padparadscha, peach-pink, black salt and pepper, fancy intense/vivid pink, fancy yellow.
- Metal pairings seen: platinum head with 14k rose shank; platinum with 18k yellow; yellow-gold head on platinum shank; white-gold basket on yellow shank. Rose gold is the default for morganite, opal and twig designs; 14k yellow for teal sapphire and salt-and-pepper.
- Nature: twig, leaf and vine shanks pair with morganite or moissanite rounds in rose gold, usually a 4-prong or leaf head.
- Famous: Diana/Kate sapphire (12 ct oval, 14 diamonds, 18k white gold), Jackie Kennedy emerald/diamond toi-et-moi, Eugenie padparadscha (carat unpublished).
- Vocabulary gaps: kite cut is present, but no shank for pinched/bark texture, no 10k gold ids, no stone id for padparadscha/lab-alexandrite (use sapphire, alexandrite), no 'lab-grown' flag distinct from lab-diamond, no ombre/graduated accent kind, no 'multi-gem halo'.
