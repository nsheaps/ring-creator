## Takeaways

- Bezel and gold are rising. The Knot 2026 study (via National Jeweler) puts yellow gold at 39% of couples, up more than double in five years, and notes bezel and burnish-set centres growing for about three years: [National Jeweler](https://www.nationaljeweler.com/articles/14726-engagement-ring-trends-2026-what-s-in-and-why). Melissa Tyson Designs also calls bezels a 2026 trend: [MTD](https://www.melissatysondesigns.com/blogs/news/why-bezel-set-engagement-rings-are-trending-for-2026).
- Shapes: oval 25% and round 26%; emerald, marquise and elongated cushion are the fastest growing, which fits the east-west emerald and oval rows above (same National Jeweler link).
- Thicker bands: National Jeweler expects thicker bands and cigar-band shanks to continue; JCK says the wide, chunky look has overtaken the whisper-thin solitaire: [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/chunky-is-the-new-classic).
- Unconventional cuts (kite, hexagon, shield, rose-cut) are mostly sold by small studios in 14k yellow gold bezels, with natural stones in the $4,500-$9,000 range in one retailer's listings: [Point No Point Studio](https://pointnopointstudio.com/products/1-48-carat-icy-white-kite-diamond-engagement-ring-bezel-jules-setting-14k-yellow-gold). I found no evidence they are mass-market yet.
- Trillion stones: one guide says bezel or V-prong preserves the triangle: [Clean Origin](https://www.cleanorigin.com/blog/trillion-cut-diamond-guide/).
- Tension settings are mostly platinum, setting-only prices about $2,100-$3,000 in this sample; several James Allen tension styles are sold out.
- Toi et moi is widely offered in lab diamond at $1,200-$1,750 (VRAI, Diamond Nexus).
- Gaps: I could not retrieve Brilliant Earth, Catbird or Mejuri engagement-ring prices; The Who What Wear 2026 trends page returned only a title.
